Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Spencheck account and all associated data at any time, either from the app or from here — you don't need the app installed to request it.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes your Firestore backup data, your Firebase Authentication account, and (if the app is installed on the device you're using) every local table on that device.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Spencheck app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete account".
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and all cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Email us from your registered account email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request." We'll verify the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Email mgokulnarayana@gmail.com

What gets deleted

Your Firestore backup (categories, expenses, lending records, budgets, credit cards, wallets, savings goals, subscriptions, loans, investments, income, recurring expenses, tags, chat history, and auto-categorisation keywords), your Firebase Authentication account (email, sign-in credentials), and any shared-wallet entries you own.

What isn't affected

Local data on a device where you haven't also tapped "Delete account" in the app stays until you uninstall the app or delete it yourself — email-based deletion only reaches what's stored in the cloud (Firebase), since there's no way to reach a device remotely.
