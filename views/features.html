Expense Tracking

Log every rupee, effortlessly

Adding an expense takes under 5 seconds. Type an amount, pick a category (or let Spencheck suggest one), and save. The app handles the rest.

✓
Auto-categorisation — Type "Swiggy" and Spencheck instantly suggests Food. Uses your own history first, then custom keywords, then a built-in dictionary of Indian brands.
✓
Receipt OCR — Photograph a receipt and the app reads the amount and merchant automatically using on-device ML.
✓
Payment method tracking — Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, or Credit Card. UPI logs the specific app (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, etc.).
✓
Cashback recording — Log cashback earned on Card or UPI payments, visible in Reports.
✓
GST tagging — Mark an expense as GST-inclusive or GST-on-top at 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% — ideal for freelancers and consultants.
✓
Duplicate detection — Warns you if a similar expense (same category, same day, similar amount) already exists, so you never double-log by accident.
✓
Unusual spend alert — Get an instant notification if a single expense is ≥30% of your monthly budget limit.

Android Only

SMS auto-detection — banking, handled

On Android, Spencheck reads your bank debit SMS alerts and turns them into expenses — so you never have to manually log a bank transaction again.

✓
On-device parsing — Uses regex + an on-device ML model ("Needle 2") to extract amount, merchant, and UPI app. No data leaves your phone.
✓
Smart review queue — New merchants appear in a "Pending" card on the Dashboard. Repeat merchants are added silently with an undo option.
✓
Card & wallet matching — The last 4 digits in an SMS (e.g. "Card ending 1234") auto-match to your saved credit cards or bank wallets.
✓
Home screen widget integration — Log preset common expenses (₹50 coffee, ₹15 chai) from your Android home screen in one tap — no app needed.

Security & Privacy

Fort Knox for your finances

Spencheck was designed with privacy as a constraint, not a feature. Your money data stays on your device, protected by multiple layers of security.

✓
Local-first storage — All data is in a SQLite database on your device. No account required to use the app.
✓
PIN lock with biometrics — Set a 4-digit PIN that locks the app on close or when it goes to the background. Enable fingerprint or Face ID for quick unlocking.
✓
Lockout after failed attempts — 3 wrong PINs trigger a 30-second lockout that doubles with each further failure. No brute-forcing.
✓
Secure PIN storage — The PIN is stored in Android Keystore, not in plain local storage.
✓
Hidden from screenshots & screen recording — On Android, the app is blocked from screenshots, screen recording, and the recent-apps preview by default, since your balances and card details are sensitive. Toggle it off anytime from Security & backup if you want to take a screenshot yourself.
✓
Complete data deletion — "Delete account" removes your Firestore data, Firebase Auth account, and every local row — in one confirmed step.
✓
Optional cloud backup — Firebase backup is strictly opt-in. The app never syncs anything automatically until you explicitly sign in and enable it.
